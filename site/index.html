Install and start playing

  1. Download your edition

    Choose Plus for two Android displays, or Plus SS for a single display. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser.

  2. Add your own games

    Open Settings, add your ROM folder and select a game from the library. No commercial game ROMs or BIOS files are supplied.

  3. Add a companion

    For a supported ROM, use “Find and create companion” in its Companion pack menu, or import a ZIP from the browser Builder.

Common questions

What is the difference between GBA Duo Plus and Plus SS?

Plus displays its companion on a second Android screen. Plus SS embeds the game and companion on one screen with touch controls. They install side by side and keep separate private saves, packs and settings.

Does every game have a companion?

Companion packs are authored for specific games and ROM revisions. The emulator supports GBA, GB and GBC games; automatic companion creation is limited to the exact profiles listed by the Companion Builder supported-games list.

Is this the original GBA Duo?

GBA Duo Plus is an independent community fork maintained by levi-soft, based on GBA Duo by khumam and contributors. It is not an official or endorsed GBA Duo release.
